## Description and exit criteria

Re-pointed three times on 2026-10-26, each time so milestone ids keep
following release order (doc-10): from 0.7.0 (UI toolkit, [m-6](m-6%20-%200.7.0-%E2%80%94-ui-toolkit-parameter-kinds%2C-curated-compon.md)),
from 0.8.0 (agent access, [m-7](m-7%20-%200.8.0-%E2%80%94-agent-access-loopback-transport%2C-mcp-serve.md)) and from 0.9.0 (hosts,
[m-8](m-8%20-%200.9.0-%E2%80%94-hosts-qgis-server-plugins%2C-standalone-pyqg.md); services II and layer coverage take 0.10.0 and 0.11.0).
[TASK-10](../tasks/task-10%20-%20Prototype-an-optional-Rust-algorithm-backend-after-v1.md) has always said "after v1", so the reservation now sits after the
1.0.0 the API-stability decision will define. A deferred research
reservation must not share a number with a release that ships.

Optional future research only after a fresh go/no-go decision; no commitment to
Rust shipping. Nothing here is scheduled, and nothing here is a reason to hold
a release.

Exit criteria, both required **if, and only if, the research is revived**:

- **`qgis-sdk`** — a prototype backend ([TASK-10](../tasks/task-10%20-%20Prototype-an-optional-Rust-algorithm-backend-after-v1.md)) is evaluated against the
  qualified reference workflow and the outcome, including a no-go, is
  recorded; the gate is [TASK-71](../tasks/task-71%20-%20Prepare-and-qualify-1.1.0-release-tentative-deferred-target-post-1.0.md).
- **`@archont561/qgis-sdk`** — the revival states its bridge impact
  explicitly: either "none, wire contract unchanged" or the contract version
  bump the prototype forces. A revival that cannot answer this does not start.

## Version policy

1.1.0 is a SemVer planning target, not a published version, until its gate is
authorised. Experimental 0.x breaking changes require a documented minor
release; patches are compatible fixes. Prereleases may use `1.1.0-rc.N` for a
pipeline rehearsal (dry run, no uploads). Do not bump manifests during
planning. `qgis-sdk`, `@archont561/qgis-sdk` and `@archont561/qgis-sdk-ui`
carry **independent** versions, each derived from its own commits and stamped
by its own release tag ([decision-25](../decisions/decision-25%20-%20Version-each-package-independently-behind-a-shared-contract-version.md)); nothing is released while this milestone stays tentative. Stable 1.0.0 requires a later
explicit API-stability decision.

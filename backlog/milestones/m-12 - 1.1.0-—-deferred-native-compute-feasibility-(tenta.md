## Description and exit criteria

Re-pointed four times on 2026-10-26, each time so milestone ids keep
following release order (doc-10): from 0.7.0 ([m-6](m-6%20-%200.7.0-%E2%80%94-ui-toolkit-parameter-kinds%2C-curated-compon.md)), 0.8.0
([m-7](m-7%20-%200.8.0-%E2%80%94-plugin-http-access%2C-mcp-serve.md)), 0.9.0 ([m-8](m-8%20-%200.9.0-%E2%80%94-hosts-qgis-server-plugins%2C-standalone-pyqg.md)) and finally from `m-11` when
the 1.0.0 readiness milestone ([m-11](m-11%20-%201.0.0-%E2%80%94-stable-api%2C-support-window%2C-distribution%2C-.md)) took that id.
[TASK-10](../tasks/task-10%20-%20Prototype-an-optional-Rust-algorithm-backend-after-v1.md) has always said "after v1", so the reservation sits after 1.0.0. A
deferred research reservation must not share a number with a release that
ships.

Optional future research only after a fresh go/no-go decision; no commitment to
Rust shipping. Nothing here is scheduled, and nothing here is a reason to hold
a release.

Exit criteria, both required **if, and only if, the research is revived**:

- **`qgis-sdk`** — a prototype backend ([TASK-10](../tasks/task-10%20-%20Prototype-an-optional-Rust-algorithm-backend-after-v1.md)) is evaluated against the
  qualified reference workflow and the outcome, including a no-go, is
  recorded; the gate is [TASK-71](../tasks/task-71%20-%20Prepare-and-qualify-1.1.0-release-tentative-deferred-target-post-1.0.md).
- **`@archont561/qgis-sdk`** — the revival states its bridge impact
  explicitly: either "none, wire contract unchanged" or the contract version
  bump the prototype forces. A revival that cannot answer this does not start.

A prototyped result shape that leaks a native handle would be a bridge
contract change, not an implementation detail, and would be refused under
[decision-9](../decisions/decision-9%20-%20Declare-allowlisted-typed-bridge-endpoints-with-runtime-owned-routers.md). No artifact is stamped or published while the milestone stays
tentative; after 1.0 it would also be a breaking change under [decision-56](../decisions/decision-56%20-%20Define-1.0-API-stability-as-tiers-with-a-generated-public-API-inventory-and-a-one-minor-deprecation-window.md).

## Version policy

1.1.0 is a SemVer planning target, not a published version, until its gate is
authorised. After 1.0 a breaking change in a stable symbol requires a major
([decision-56](../decisions/decision-56%20-%20Define-1.0-API-stability-as-tiers-with-a-generated-public-API-inventory-and-a-one-minor-deprecation-window.md)); a minor adds only. Prereleases may use `1.1.0-rc.N` for a
pipeline rehearsal (dry run, no uploads). Do not bump manifests during
planning. `qgis-sdk`, `@archont561/qgis-sdk` and `@archont561/qgis-sdk-ui`
carry **independent** versions, each derived from its own commits and stamped
by its own release tag ([decision-25](../decisions/decision-25%20-%20Version-each-package-independently-behind-a-shared-contract-version.md)); nothing is released while this milestone
stays tentative.

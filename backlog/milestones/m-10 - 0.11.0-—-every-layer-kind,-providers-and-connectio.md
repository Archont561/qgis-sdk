## Description and exit criteria

labelling and the style manager ([TASK-93](../tasks/task-93%20-%20Add-symbology-labelling-and-the-style-manager-renderer-and-symbol-models-labelling-settings-style-manager-read-QML-and-SLD-import-and-export.md)), and Processing breadth -

([doc-22](../docs/plans/doc-22%20-%20Service-coverage-map.md)) shows every listed area complete or
out of scope by decision, which is what "all services and layers covered"
means before 1.0.

Exit criteria, both required:

- **`qgis-sdk`** — released at 0.11.0 from one green gate ([TASK-95](../tasks/task-95%20-%20Prepare-and-qualify-0.11.0-release.md)) with
  the four services complete under the six-part rule; doc-12's 0.11.0
  notes; doc-22 complete.
- **`@archont561/qgis-sdk`** — **next minor** from the same gate with the
  regenerated host manifest and types; wire unchanged; shown by
  `verify-versions` and the fingerprint check.

## Version policy

0.11.0 is a SemVer planning target, not a published version, until its gate is
authorised. Experimental 0.x breaking changes require a documented minor
release; patches are compatible fixes. Prereleases may use `0.11.0-rc.N` for a
pipeline rehearsal (dry run, no uploads). Do not bump manifests during
planning. `qgis-sdk`, `@archont561/qgis-sdk` and `@archont561/qgis-sdk-ui`
carry **independent** versions, each derived from its own commits and stamped
by its own release tag ([decision-25](../decisions/decision-25%20-%20Version-each-package-independently-behind-a-shared-contract-version.md)); the bridge minor is paired by the shared plugin-description contract version and the regenerated host manifest. Stable 1.0.0 requires a later
explicit API-stability decision.

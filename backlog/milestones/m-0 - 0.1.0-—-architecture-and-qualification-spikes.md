## Description and exit criteria

QGIS-free contracts and deterministic declarations, the validator and scheduler
spikes, and the first published **pair**: `qgis-sdk` and `qgis-sdk-bridge` carry the same 0.1.x numbers because their
surfaces were built and released together, not because a policy required it;
from 0.2.0 they version independently ([decision-25](../decisions/decision-25%20-%20Version-each-package-independently-behind-a-shared-contract-version.md)). The plugin-description
contract version, not the package number, is what pairs a release. Stable 1.0.0 requires a later
explicit API-stability decision.
